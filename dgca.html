DGCA Computer
Number Process

To become a pilot, obtaining a DGCA Computer Number is essential. This unique ID is required for all DGCA exams, enabling you to track your application status and exam results, and it’s crucial for your pilot license.

The application involves filling out a form on the DGCA website and submitting the necessary documents. We guide you through every step, so you don’t waste time guessing.

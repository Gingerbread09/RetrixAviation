Medical Fitness to Be a Pilot

  • Good vision (with or without spectacles)
  • Normal hearing
  • No major medical conditions
  • Stable mental health

Where to Get Medicals

Available in Kochi, Ernakulam, Kerala, and Trivandrum through DGCA-approved doctors.
